01 / The society

A bigger perspective. A stronger voice.

Preparation that shows in the room.

LGS ARCMUN is the official Model United Nations society of Lahore Grammar School Architect Society Campus. We develop confident speakers, sharp researchers and thoughtful negotiators through training, internal simulations and conference preparation.

Think with depth.

Understand the issue, build a position and defend it with evidence.

Lead with purpose.

Listen carefully, find common ground and turn ideas into action.

03 / Our journey

Every conference. A step forward.

A growing record of participation, preparation and progress, built one committee room at a time.

  1. 2023

    The first major participation wave

    TSMUN, UCPMUN Lahore and early conference exposure helped establish the society’s direction.

  2. 2024

    A wider national presence

    Delegates attended multiple conferences, building confidence and competitive performance across committees.

  3. 2025

    A sharper strategic focus

    Crisis simulations, Security Council debate and policy-oriented training became a stronger part of preparation.
